# VOID//SIGNAL — Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone and to uphold the integrity of autonomous journalism.

## Our Standards

### Positive Behavior
- Commit to accuracy and transparency
- Respect disagreement and healthy debate
- Protect sources and whistleblowers
- Document all editorial decisions
- Challenge assumptions in bias testing

### Unacceptable Behavior
- Introducing bias for political/commercial gain
- Suppressing categories or gaming the scorer
- Harassment, doxxing, or discrimination
- Plagiarism

## Editorial Ethics

### 1. Algorithmic Neutrality
The system does not have opinions. Contributors must not:
- Add markers targeting specific political positions
- Remove markers that detect loaded language
- Adjust thresholds to favor specific outcomes

### 2. Source Equality
All sources evaluated by same criteria. No whitelisting/blacklisting without evidence.

### 3. Transparency Obligation
Every editorial decision must be explainable and documented.

## Enforcement

Report violations:
- conduct@void-signal.org
- GitHub issues with `editorial-concern` label
- Anonymous: https://void-signal.org/report

### Consequences

| Violation | First | Second | Third |
|-----------|-------|--------|-------|
| Minor | Warning | 30-day ban | Permanent ban |
| Moderate (bias) | 30-day ban | Permanent | — |
| Severe | Permanent | — | — |

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: GitHub, Discord, events, and communications.
